Profile

Managing Partner · Advocate of the High Court of Kenya · Commissioner for Oaths

Cosmas Mureti leads Mureti Cosmas & Co. Advocates in Mombasa. His publicly listed areas of work include family law, property law, corporate law, bankruptcy, immigration, wills planning, criminal defence and labour and employment law.

He is listed by the Law Society of Kenya under practising number P.105/20569/22 and as a member of the Society's Criminal Justice Committee. The Kenya Gazette also records his appointment as a Commissioner for Oaths.
